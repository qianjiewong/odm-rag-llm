Making illegal things legal.

Accelerate Estonia is a governmental innovation lab shaping public policy to enable impactful businesses.

What do we do?

We unlock new markets. Whether it’s restrictive laws, outdated regulations, or burdensome bureaucracy – we see these roadblocks as opportunities for change. As we remove barriers to innovation, we foresee a ripple effect — new market opportunities emerge, not just for businesses in Estonia, but also beyond.

We create policy recommendations for the Estonian government that help companies overcome regulatory barriers and bring innovative solutions to the market.

Our value offer

  • Entrepreneurs

    Accelerate Estonia ensures the regulatory needs of businesses reach the right government officials. We drive legislative change: by gathering relevant public and private sector stakeholders, we collaboratively seek solutions to legislative bottlenecks that the public sector can implement.
    Read more

  • Public Sector

    Government officials, often overwhelmed with ideas from entrepreneurs, can lean on us to test and validate new policy ideas brought forward by the private sector. We help alleviate public sector workload by evaluating and forwarding promising proposals, allowing data-based decision-making.
    Read more

  • Partners

    Accelerate Estonia partners with leading incubators, accelerators, universities, and organisations to help grow innovative businesses in their portfolios, by guiding them through regulatory hurdles and validating their solutions with top-level leaders in the public sector.
    Read more

Our workflow

More detailed description

  • Validation

    1 - 3 months

    Is there a need for regulatory change?

  • Definition

    3 - 6 months

    Which legislation requires modification, and how can the proposed changes be achieved?

  • Proof

    6 - 18 months

    Prove the necessity of the proposed legislative change by way of e.g. a pilot.

  • Proposal

    Aftercare

    Propose a legislative amendment to the responsible ministry and support in following through with the amendment.
