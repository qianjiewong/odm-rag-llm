Deep Forests

Forests significantly shape our way of thinking. Timber is a key export for us, and books celebrating our forests consistently top the bestseller lists.

Private Spaces

Estonia has a low population density and plenty of untouched nature. Forests and bogs blanket three-quarters of the country's landscape.

Clean Environment

Estonia is one of only seven countries with air clean enough to meet World Health Organisation guidelines. Our water is consistently clean, ideal for both swimming and drinking.
