Benefits of becoming an e-resident

29 June 2026 07:38 Updated

Estonia's e-Residency programme gives entrepreneurs worldwide a secure digital identity and access to Estonia's digital business environment. With it, you can run a trusted EU company entirely online - no matter where you live. 

E-Residency works best if you run a location-independent business, offer digital or remote services such as IT, marketing, or consulting, or want to operate in the EU market without being physically based there. It is especially useful if you work as a freelancer, consultant, or digital nomad, or if you manage a remote or international team and need a simple way to run your company online.

 

As an e-resident, you can

  • Register and manage an EU company fully online.
  • Sign documents and contracts digitally.
  • Access a wide range of Estonian public and private e-services.
  • Work with trusted service providers on our Marketplace.
  • Join a community of 130 000+ entrepreneurs worldwide.
  • Apply for both Estonian and European funding opportunities.

Four main benefits for entrepreneurs

1. Ease

Easily set up and manage your company entirely online.

  • No need to travel to Estonia to register or manage your company
  • Handle company management, accounting, and compliance remotely with digital tools and service providers
    • See how to reduce bureaucracy on our blog
  • Reduce time spent on administrative tasks and focus more on running your business
  • For example, a freelance UX designer based in Portugal can register an Estonian company, invoice clients in the EU and US, and manage accounting entirely online through a service provider, without needing to visit Estonia

 

2. Trust

Build credibility with your customers and partners by running your business through a transparent and regulated EU framework.

  • Increase credibility with clients by operating a company registered in the EU
    • Companies registered in Estonia can also benefit from the EU regulations such as the free movement of goods and services
  • Benefit from a transparent business environment with clear legal and reporting rules
    • Company information is verified and publicly accessible, making due diligence easier for entrepreneurs, investors, and partners
    • International indexes consistently rank Estonia among the top countries worldwide for transparency, rule of law, low corruption, and digital security
  • Use internationally recognized secure digital systems for company administration and document signing
  • For example, a freelance marketing consultant based in Thailand works with clients in Germany and the US. Having an Estonian company helps them present a professional EU-based business structure, making it easier to sign contracts and work with international clients.

 

3. Opportunity

Grow your business internationally and access new markets through a trusted EU company structure.

  • Access the EU market through an EU-registered company structure 
  • Work with international clients and expand beyond your local market 
  • Scale your business globally while operating fully online 
  • Collaborate with partners, freelancers, and teams across different countries
  • Access a curated Marketplace of trusted service providers for accounting, legal support, banking, and company management
  • For example, a small SaaS founder based in Argentina can register an Estonian company, sell subscriptions to customers across Europe and North America, and manage the entire business remotely while working with a distributed development team.

 

4. Community

Join a global community of like-minded entrepreneurs and founders.

  • Connect with other e-residents, founders, and service providers around the world
  • Learn from shared experiences of people running remote and international businesses
  • Find practical support and guidance for building and managing your company
  • Take part of our e-resident networking events
  • For example, a first-time founder based in Indonesia joins an online e-resident community, where they get advice from other entrepreneurs on choosing an accounting provider and setting up their first EU company, helping them avoid common early-stage mistakes

 

Learn from the community

Sometimes to best way to understand the benefits of e-Residency is to learn from those who are already building their businesses. 

  • Explore our blog. Read stories from e-residents across the globe. You can filter by region, industry, or goal to find most relevant insights.
  • Connect with envoys and community leaders. They are expert in their fields and successful entrepreneurs. They often host events for the community around the world and foster connections with media, prospective e-residents and also represent e-residents with policymakers.
  • Engage on social media.  Connect with other e-residents, share experiences, and get support from the wider community. 

 

Not limited to entrepreneurs

Many people apply for e-Residency to access Estonia's digital services, even if they do not run a business. Common examples include:

  • People with personal connections to Estonia, such as a family member
  • Researchers collaborating with Estonian institutions
  • Professionals working with Estonian companies
  • Former residents who wish to keep access to e-services

These e-residents value Estonia's secure digital solutions for their work and personal needs.

 

Important to know

  • E-Residency does not guarantee a bank account or payment service. Access depends on the individual terms of each provider. See our article Banking basics.
  • E-Residency is not physical residency and does not affect your personal tax residency.
  • E-Residency is not an immigration program and does not grant visa, residence permit, or any right to live in or enter Estonia or the EU. It is not a pathway to residency or citizenship. See What is e-Residency?

FAQ

Can I travel to the EU with e-Residency?
No. Your status as an e-resident does not grant permission to travel to Estonia or within the EU without a visa, should one be needed. Your e-Residency digital ID card can not be used for travel and does not display a photo for physical identification.
Can I live in the EU with e-Residency?
No. Your status as an e-resident does not grant permission to live in Estonia or within the EU. You will not receive benefits related to EU residency by becoming an e-resident.
Will e-Residency affect my current residency, citizenship or visas?
E-Residency does not have any effect on your current residency, citizenship or visas in any country.
Will I get a physical address in Estonia with e-Residency?
No. Your e-Residency status itself does not come with a physical address in Estonia. To register your company in Estonia, you will likely need to use a business service provider to provide contact person and legal address service.
Will I get a bank account with e-Residency?
You need to apply separately for a banking account depending on your business needs. Banking services are provided by private companies. While the e-Residency programme works closely with both public and private sector partners to support our community, only the service providers themselves can decide whether to approve your application. e-Residency cannot guarantee access to a bank account for personal use. For business banking, providers typically evaluate your application based on your company’s business model and operations.
