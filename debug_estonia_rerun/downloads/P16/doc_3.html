Need help? Here's who to contact!

26 June 2026 12:58 Updated

E-Residency is an Estonian government initiative supported by several organisations that assist e-residents at different stages of their journey. 

This guide helps you quickly find the right place to get assistance.

Please contact all institutions in English.

 

Main contacts

For questions about the e-Residency programme

e-Residency customer support

Ask us about:
  • the programme
  • application process
  • Estonian business environment
  • general information on business formation and taxation
  • benefits and opportunities

Please note that business consultation as well as tax and legal advice can only be sought from private providers.

Contact us via Contact form. 

NB! Please make sure to verify your email address after submitting the form.

 

For questions regarding your e-Residency application or status

The Estonian Police and Border Guard Board (PBGB)

Contact PBGB if you need help with:
  • technical issues when submitting your application
  • get an error notification within the application environment
  • making changes to your application (e.g. details or pick-up location)
  • payments, receipts, refunds, or failed transactions
  • eligibility requirements, restrictions, or rejected applications (including appeals)
  • delays in processing (e.g. if no decision has been made within 30 days)
  • your e-Residency account (e.g. account deletion)
  • collecting your e-Residency kit or confirming your pick-up location
  • questions about mobile pick-up locations, including schedules, locations, and other matters
  • closing your e-Residency or if your status has been revoked

TIP: Be sure you provide PBGB with your full name and date of birth or the application number so they have the information needed to advise you. 

Email: ppa@politsei.ee

State helpline: +372 600 1247

Check your application status in the e-Residency application environment.

 

For questions about collecting your e-Residency digital ID

Estonian embassies and pickup locations

Contact your pickup location directly if you need to: 
  • book or change your appointment to collect your digital ID 
  • check whether your card has arrived or is still available for collection
  • request an extension to your pick-up deadline

Find contacts at our pickup locations page. 

Please note that you can also check the details of your e-Residency digital ID delivery at My e-Residency portal.

 

For help using your digital ID

ID Software user support

Visit their website for software, guidance, and troubleshooting on:
  • installing and using ID software
  • using your digital ID card (including PIN and PUK codes)
  • setting up and checking compatibility of smart card readers
  • logging into Estonian e-services
  • signing and encrypting documents (e.g. with DigiDoc)
  • resolving technical issues (e.g. card not detected, reader not found, or card chip errors)

Call the ID helpline +372 677 3377 if you need to suspend your certificates (e.g. if your card has been lost or stolen)

Contact them via their Contact form  

 

For information about Estonian taxes

The Estonian Tax and Customs Board (EMTA)

Contact the Estonian Tax and Customs Board (EMTA) if you need help with:
  • understanding the Estonian tax system
  • specific questions about Estonian VAT
  • using the e-Tax system
  • requesting a consultation

Visit the EMTA homepage

Email: nonresident@emta.ee 

 

For help with your company

Business Service providers

Visit the e‑Residency Marketplace to find service providers who can help you with:
  • company registration and legal address services
  • contact person and compliance support
  • opening and managing a business bank account
  • accounting, annual reports, and VAT registration
  • business consulting, tax advice, and legal services
  • specialised support for growth (e.g. acceleration programmes, marketing, or industry‑specific needs)
  • tailored service packages that combine multiple business services to fit your needs

Find the selection of services and service providers on our Marketplace.
